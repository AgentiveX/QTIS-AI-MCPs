# Read-only prompts

Choose the practice after listing the ones your account can access.

## Check the connection

> Use QTIS Patients & Clients to list the practices I can access. Do not create
> or change any records.

## Browse current profiles

> In my selected practice, show up to five active patients with their names,
> case file numbers, and statuses. Do not change any records.

## Find a profile

> Find the patient by the case file number I provide in my selected practice.
> If there is more than one match, ask me which profile to use.

## Review consultation profiles

> List consultation patients in my selected practice. Tell me if more pages
> are available. Do not update their status.

## Inspect archives

> Show the archived patient profiles in my selected practice. Do not restore
> or change any records.

## Check available locations

> Show the active locations I can assign to patient profiles in my selected practice.
